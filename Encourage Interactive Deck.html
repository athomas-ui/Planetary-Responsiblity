Encourage

Five gifts. Twenty-five actions. One choice.

Encouragement is not only something we say. It is something we show through how we understand people, build their confidence, earn their trust, share knowledge, and communicate.

Why it matters

Think of the last time someone believed in you first.

Pause and reflect. Who said something that helped you move forward when you were stuck? What did they say, and what did you do next?

Encouragement changes outcomes. People speak up sooner, take smarter risks, and recover faster from mistakes. Encouragement is not soft. It is leadership with a purpose.
